How to use the Loan EMI Calculator

Enter the loan amount, the annual interest rate as a percentage, and the time in months, then press Calculate EMI. The tool uses the standard reducing-balance EMI formula, where the monthly rate is the annual rate divided by 12, and shows the monthly payment, the total amount you will pay, and the total interest.

Example

A loan of 100,000 at 12 percent per year over 12 months gives a monthly EMI of about 8,884.88, a total payment of about 106,618.56 and total interest of about 6,618.56. This is an estimate only: it does not include bank fees, charges or taxes, and a real loan offer may differ, so always confirm the figures with the lender.

Paying extra

Paying a little extra each month, when your loan allows it, cuts the total interest more than most people expect, because extra payments go straight to the balance that interest is charged on. Even a small regular overpayment can shorten a long loan by months. Before doing this, check with your lender that there is no early repayment charge and that extra payments are applied to the balance, not held as a future payment.
