How to use the Percentage Calculator

The tool covers three common percentage questions. To find a percentage of a number, enter the percentage and the number. To find what percentage one number is of another, enter both numbers. To find a percentage increase or decrease, enter the old and new values. Each mode shows the working, so you can check the answer by hand.

Example

Twenty percent of 250 is 50, because 250 times 20 divided by 100 equals 50. If a price rises from 80 to 100, the increase is 20, which is 25 percent of 80. Percentages are used for discounts, tips, marks, interest and price rises, and the same formula works for all of them: part divided by whole, times 100.

Common mistakes

The most common percentage mistake is using the wrong whole. A rise from 80 to 100 is a 25 percent increase, because the increase of 20 is compared with the starting value of 80, but a fall from 100 to 80 is a 20 percent decrease, because the fall of 20 is compared with 100. Always ask: percent of what? If the whole changes, the percentage changes too, even when the amount stays the same.
