How to use the Unit Converter

Choose what you want to convert, enter a number, and pick the from and to units. The tool converts length, weight and temperature instantly, so you can switch between metric and imperial units without looking up a table. For temperature, remember that Celsius and Fahrenheit do not share the same zero point, so the tool uses the proper formula rather than a simple multiplication.

Example

One kilometre is 1,000 metres and about 0.62 miles, one kilogram is about 2.20 pounds, and 20 degrees Celsius is 68 degrees Fahrenheit. These conversions are useful for recipes, travel, homework and online shopping from another country. Results are rounded for display, so for engineering or medical work, double-check with a specialist converter.

Rounding

Converted results are rounded for display, which is fine for cooking, travel and homework, but small rounding differences can add up if you convert a long chain of units one step at a time. For the most accurate result, convert directly from the starting unit to the final unit in one step, and keep extra decimal places until the last step. If a converted measurement must be exact, such as a medicine dose, use a specialist source and check it twice.
