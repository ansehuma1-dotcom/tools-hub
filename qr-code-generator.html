QR Code Generator — Tools Hub

Type a link or any text, pick a size, and generate. The code itself is made in your browser; a small QR library is loaded once from a public CDN to draw it.

How to use the QR Code Generator

Type or paste a link or a short piece of text, then generate the QR code and download it as a PNG image. The code is created in your browser, so your link or text is not sent to a server. Print the image on a flyer, poster or business card, and test it by scanning it with a phone camera before you print a large batch.

Example

A shop can put a QR code linking to its website on a receipt, and a teacher can put one linking to a worksheet on the classroom wall. Make the code at least 2 centimetres across when printed, keep a quiet white border around it, and check that it still scans from the distance people will actually stand at.

Where QR codes work best

QR codes work best where people have a phone in hand and a reason to scan, such as a poster in a shop, a handout at an event, or a business card. They work badly on fast-moving vehicles, tiny labels, or pages where the link is already clickable, because scanning adds effort for no benefit. Always label what the code leads to, such as a menu or a booking page, so people know whether it is worth scanning.
