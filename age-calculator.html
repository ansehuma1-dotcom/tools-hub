Age Calculator — Tools Hub

Pick your date of birth to get your age in years, months and days.

How to use the Age Calculator

Pick your date of birth from the calendar field, then press Calculate Age. The tool compares that date with today and works out your age in years, months and days, borrowing days and months correctly when the day or month has not been reached yet this year. A date in the future is rejected, because it cannot give a real age.

Example

Someone born on 1 January 2000 is 26 years old on 1 January 2026. If today is earlier in the year than their birthday, the tool shows one year less plus the completed months and days since the last birthday. The result uses your device date, so check that your device date is correct if the answer looks wrong by a day.

Leap years

The calculator handles leap years correctly, because it works with real calendar dates rather than assuming every year has 365 days. Someone born on 29 February celebrates a birthday in non-leap years on either 28 February or 1 March, depending on local custom, but their age in years still increases on that date. If you need an age for a legal or official purpose, use the date on the official document, not a rounded age in years alone.
