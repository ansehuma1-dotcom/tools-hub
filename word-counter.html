How to use the Word Counter and Case Converter

Paste or type your text into the box, and the tool counts words, characters, sentences and paragraphs as you work. You can then change the whole text to upper case, lower case or title case with one click, which is handy for fixing headings, titles and pasted notes. Words are split on spaces, so extra spaces and blank lines can change the count slightly.

Example

The sentence "Hello world, this is a test." has 6 words and 28 characters including spaces. Students use the counter for essays with a word limit, writers use it for headlines, and teachers use it to check a passage length before printing. The text stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

What counts as a word

Different counters can disagree by a few words, because they treat hyphenated words, numbers and contractions differently. Some count well-known as one word and others as two, and some count a number such as 2026 as a word while others do not. If a word limit is strict, such as a university essay, check your institution's own rules and use its counter for the final count, and use this tool for drafting and quick checks.
