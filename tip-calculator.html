How to use the Tip Calculator

Enter the bill amount, choose a tip percentage, and say how many people are splitting the bill. The tool shows the tip amount, the total bill including the tip, and the cost per person, so a group can settle fairly without arguing over the maths. In the USA, a restaurant tip of 15 to 20 percent is common, but the right amount depends on the service and the local custom.

Example

A bill of 120 with a 20 percent tip adds 24, making a total of 144. Split between 4 people, that is 36 each. If one person ordered far more than the others, split the food bill by what each person ordered and share only the tip equally, or agree the split before ordering to avoid an awkward end to the meal.

Tipping customs differ

Tipping customs differ by country and by type of service, so the percentage that is generous in one place may be unusual in another. In some countries a service charge is already included in the bill, in which case an extra tip is optional, while in others tips are the main part of a server's income. When travelling, check the local custom and the bill itself before tipping, and tip in cash if you want to be sure it reaches the server.
