Book Matchmaker — Tools Hub

Tell us who is looking, pick an age band and interests, and get 4 tailored book recommendations with original short summaries and steps to find them at the library. This is a template matcher from a small curated list — it is not a library catalogue and it does not use AI, so check availability at your own library.

How to use the Book Matchmaker

Say who is looking for the book, a student, teacher or parent, then pick an age band from 6-8, 9-11, 12-14 or 15 plus, and tick up to three interests such as Adventure, Mystery, Fantasy or Animals. The tool filters its curated list to books that fit the age band, ranks them by how many of your interests they match, and shows four recommendations with an original short summary, themes, and steps to find each book at the library.

Example

A 9 to 11 year old who likes Adventure and Fantasy might be shown Peter Pan and The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe, both of which match both interests. At the library, search the catalogue by title and author, ask for the Children's Fiction section, and reserve the book if it is checked out, or try the next book on the list.
