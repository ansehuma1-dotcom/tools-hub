About Tools Hub

Tools Hub is a small collection of free, everyday online tools. It follows the same idea as the EduGems library — a clear list, grouped by category, where you pick what you need — but here every item is a working tool, not just a prompt.

Each tool runs in your browser. There is no account to create, no API key to enter, and your inputs are not sent to a server.

More tools will be added only when they are finished and tested.

What you will find here

The tools are grouped into Calculators, Converters, Text Tools, Generators, Education, Finance, Business and SEO Tools. Calculators cover everyday maths such as BMI, age, loan payments, discounts, percentages, tips, budgets and mortgages. Education tools help teachers build assessments, lesson plans, practice questions and parent messages. Every tool follows the same rule: open it, enter your numbers or text, and get an answer at once, on a phone, tablet or computer.

Who it is for

Tools Hub is for anyone who needs a quick, honest answer without installing an app or creating an account: students checking homework, teachers planning a lesson, shop owners writing an invoice, and families working out a budget. If a tool cannot do something, its page says so plainly, so you are not left guessing.
