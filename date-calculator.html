How to use the Date Calculator

The tool answers three date questions: how many days are between two dates, how many business days fall between two dates, and what date you get when you add or subtract a number of days from a date. Enter the dates in year, month and day order and check them carefully, because one wrong digit changes the whole answer.

Example

From 1 January 2026 to 1 February 2026 is 31 days. Adding 90 days to 1 January 2026 gives 1 April 2026. Business-day counts skip Saturdays and Sundays, which is useful for delivery estimates, school deadlines and work schedules, but they do not skip public holidays, so allow extra days around a holiday.

Dates only, no times

This calculator works with calendar dates only and does not handle times of day or time zones. Two dates in different time zones can be a different number of days apart depending on where you measure from, so for travel or deadline planning, confirm the date in the time zone that matters. The calculator also counts the difference between dates, not including both end dates, so add one day if you need an inclusive count that includes the start and end dates.
