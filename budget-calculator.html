Enter your monthly income and spending. This is a planning estimate, not financial advice.

How to use the Budget Calculator

Enter your monthly income after tax, then list your spending under needs, wants and savings, or use the 50/30/20 rule as a starting point: about 50 percent for needs such as rent, food and transport, 30 percent for wants such as eating out and entertainment, and 20 percent for savings and debt repayment. The tool shows whether each group is over or under its target.

Example

On an income of 2,000 a month, the 50/30/20 targets are 1,000 for needs, 600 for wants and 400 for savings. If needs already take 1,300, the tool makes that gap visible, so you can either cut a want, find a cheaper need, or accept a lower savings rate for a while. A budget is a plan, not a judgement: adjust the percentages to fit real life, then track for a month before changing them again.

Reviewing your budget

Revisit your budget at the end of each month and compare what you planned with what you actually spent, because a budget that is never reviewed quickly stops matching real life. Look for one or two categories where spending was much higher than planned, and ask whether the plan was unrealistic or the spending was a one-off, such as a car repair or a holiday. Change only one thing at a time, so you can see whether the change actually helped.
