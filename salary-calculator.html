Salary & Paycheck Calculator — Tools Hub

Choose hourly or annual pay, enter your rate and a tax percentage. The tax result is only an estimate using the rate you enter — it is not a US tax calculation.

How to use the Salary and Paycheck Calculator

Convert in either direction: enter an hourly wage and weekly hours to get an annual salary, or enter an annual salary to get a paycheck. You choose an estimated tax rate, and the tool applies it to show a rough take-home figure. The tax rate you pick matters a lot, so use the rate that matches your situation rather than a guess.

Example

An hourly wage of 20 for 40 hours a week gives 800 a week, or about 41,600 a year before tax. At an estimated 20 percent tax, take-home pay is about 33,280 a year. Real paychecks also include pension, health and other deductions that this tool does not model, so treat the result as a planning estimate, not a payslip.
