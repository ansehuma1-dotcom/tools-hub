Check for Understanding Builder — Tools Hub

Enter grade, subject, topic, learning target and timing to get a menu of 3-5 classroom-ready checks, each with a prompt, facilitation moves, diagnostic look-fors, and Reteach / Clarify / Extend next steps, plus a printable exit ticket. This is an original template tool — no AI, no lesson upload, and no student data is stored.

How to use the Check for Understanding Builder

Enter the grade, subject, topic and learning target, then choose whether the check comes before, during or after instruction, plus the response tool your class will use, such as mini-whiteboards, hand signals or a digital poll. The tool picks four matching strategies from its set, each with a ready prompt, teacher facilitation moves, diagnostic look-fors, and Reteach, Clarify or Extend next steps, plus a printable exit ticket.

Example

For a Grade 6 science lesson on photosynthesis during instruction, with mini-whiteboards, you might get a whiteboard show where students draw and label the inputs and outputs. If most boards miss sunlight, reteach that step; if a few are unsure, clarify with one worked example; if all are correct, extend by asking what happens on a cloudy day.
