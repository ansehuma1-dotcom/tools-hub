Privacy Policy

Tools Hub does not require an account and does not collect the numbers or text you enter into a tool. Calculations happen in your browser.

We do not sell personal data. If advertising is added later, this page will be updated before it goes live.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address or payment details to use a tool, and we do not build a profile of you from your calculations. The site does not set tracking cookies. The only thing saved on your own device is your language choice for the site header, so the site can remember it on your next visit.

How the tools work

Each tool is a static page with JavaScript that runs in your browser. When you press a button, the calculation happens on your device. Your inputs are not uploaded, stored on a server, or shared with a third party by the tool itself. The pages are served as static files, so there is no database of user inputs behind them.

If a future tool ever needs data to leave your browser, that tool's page will say so before you use it.
