5E Lesson Plan Builder — Tools Hub

Enter grade, subject, topic, standards or objectives, and lesson duration to get a structured, student-centred 5E lesson plan with a materials list and an activity for every phase. This is an original template tool — no AI, no interview, and no student data is stored.

How to use the 5E Lesson Plan Builder

Enter the grade, subject, topic and standards, then choose a lesson length of 45, 60 or 90 minutes, the technology available, and any student needs. The tool builds an overview, a materials list, and all five phases, Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate and Evaluate, each with a teacher activity, a student activity, a quick check, and a timing. The phase timings always add up exactly to the length you chose.

Example

A 60-minute Grade 3 science lesson on photosynthesis might spend 5 minutes engaging with a question about leaves, 15 minutes exploring with real leaves, 10 minutes explaining the process, 15 minutes elaborating with a diagram, and 15 minutes evaluating with a short exit task. Edit the timings and activities to fit your class, and keep the check in each phase so you know what students understood.
